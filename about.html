About Me

Hi, I'm Chelsea.

I'm a girl who loves discovering beautiful places, cozy spots, good food, and exciting destinations.

I created ELSEWHERE as my little collection of places that caught my attention — the kind of places that make you want to take pictures, stay a little longer, and make memories.

Whether it's a quiet café, a beautiful view, or somewhere fun to spend the day with friends, I believe every place has a story waiting to be discovered.

Why ELSEWHERE?

I wanted to create a guide that feels personal, simple, and inspiring. Instead of just listing places, ELSEWHERE is about the experience — where to wander, where to pause, and where to make memories.

My recommendations are based on places I've discovered, experienced, researched, or genuinely believe are worth checking out. I want this guide to feel like a recommendation from a friend rather than a typical travel directory.
