my little list

Places to go.

Cruise by the Bay

📍 SM by the Bay Amusement Park, Mall of Asia Bay Area, Pasay

MOA Cruise by the Bay is a must-try for visitors looking to enjoy Manila Bay from a different perspective. Hop on a relaxing 15–30 minute boat ride for only ₱200 and take in beautiful coastal views, especially during sunset.

8 Balli'n Cafe

📍 2231 Revillin, Santa Ana, Manila, Metro Manila

8 Balli’n Cafe is a cozy and budget-friendly spot in the neighborhood. Grab a refreshing drink, challenge your friends to a game of 8-ball pool, and enjoy a laid-back hangout.

Makati Circuit

📍 Ayala Malls Circuit, Hippodromo St, Makati City

Circuit Makati is a vibrant destination where you can shop, dine, explore, and enjoy exciting entertainment in one place. It's a great spot to spend the day with friends or family.
