Cruise by the bay

📍SM by the Bay Amusement Park, Mall of Asia Bay Area, Seaside Boulevard, Pasay, Metro Manila, Philippines

MOA Cruise by the Bay is a must-try for visitors looking to enjoy Manila Bay from a different perspective. Hop on a relaxing 15–30 minute boat ride for only ₱200 and take in beautiful coastal views, especially during sunset. It’s a simple and affordable way to unwind while enjoying Manila’s scenic seaside atmosphere.

8 Balli'n Cafe

📍2231 Revillin, Santa Ana, Manila, Metro Manila

8 Balli’n Cafe is a cozy and budget-friendly spot in the neighborhood, offering delicious drinks and a fun place to unwind. Grab a refreshing drink, challenge your friends to a game of 8-ball pool, and enjoy a laid-back hangout perfect for catching up and spending time together.

Makati Circuit

📍 Ayala Malls Circuit, Hippodromo St, Makati City, 1207 Metro Manila

Circuit Makati is a vibrant destination where you can shop, dine, explore, and enjoy exciting entertainment in one place. From relaxing at cafés and restaurants to catching live events and exploring its open spaces, it’s a great spot to spend the day with friends or family. Whether you’re looking for food, leisure, or a fun night out, Circuit Makati offers something for every kind of traveler.
