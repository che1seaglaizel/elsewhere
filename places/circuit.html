Circuit Makati

About Circuit Makati

Circuit Makati is a vibrant destination where visitors can shop, dine, explore, and enjoy exciting entertainment all in one place. Located in the heart of Makati, it offers a mix of restaurants, shops, recreational activities, and open spaces that make it a great place to spend the day.

I recommend Circuit Makati because it is a convenient destination for friends and families looking for different activities in one location. Whether you want to enjoy a meal, go shopping, explore the area, or simply relax, Circuit Makati has something to offer.

Place Details

Address Ayala Malls Circuit, Hippodromo St, Makati City
Type Shopping, Dining, and Entertainment
Price Range Free entrance; prices vary by activity
