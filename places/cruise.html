About Cruise By The Bay

MOA Cruise by the Bay is a relaxing way to experience Manila Bay from a different perspective. Visitors can enjoy a 15–30 minute boat ride while taking in beautiful coastal views of the bay. It is especially recommended during sunset, when the scenery becomes more beautiful and the experience is more enjoyable.

This is a great activity for visitors who want to take a short break from exploring the Mall of Asia area and enjoy the fresh air and views along Manila Bay. With an affordable ticket price of around ₱200, it is also a budget-friendly activity to add to your trip.

Place Details

Address SM by the Bay Amusement Park, Mall of Asia Bay Area, Pasay
Activity 15–30 minute boat cruise
Opening Hours 3:00 PM - 10:00 PM
Price Range ₱200
Best Time to Visit Late afternoon / Sunset
